DESK ASSESSMENT OF PRACTICAL APPLICATIONS
14 October 2026

Evidence, not a customer survey or market-size estimate

The most plausible application is an auditable permission-coverage module for
existing operational resilience and third-party risk workflows. Target users
include infrastructure procurement teams, essential-service operators and
project financiers. The paper's public records show multiple prerequisites and
heterogeneous permission terms; they do not establish willingness to pay.

Existing products make a broad claim of an unserved risk-software market
untenable. ServiceNow's official Data Relationships Framework documentation
already maps business services to locations, contracts, third parties and
engagements. Its CMDB/BIA integration documentation describes automatic
dependency updates. OneTrust markets third-party risk management and cross-domain
risk governance. This artifact should therefore be positioned as a transparent
calculation and evidence schema that can integrate with established systems,
not as the first dependency-mapping or continuity platform.

Proposed service

1. Define essential service scope, capacity and acceptable interruption.
2. Obtain reviewed legal and contractual permissions and readiness evidence.
3. Calculate complete-path coverage over a declared recovery horizon.
4. Report exact boundary dates, missing evidence and responsible decision makers.
5. Revalidate after renewal, supplier change, exercise or incident.

The supplied JSON CLI blocks a coverage claim when scope, readiness or permission
terms are marked unresolved. It cannot establish that a reviewer is correct.
Automated document extraction could reduce clerical effort, but consequential
interpretation still needs qualified review. No customer interviews, production
deployment, revenue forecast or patentability determination has been performed.

Economic value hypothesis

An institution might pay for maintained evidence and coordination of obligations
because the relevant permissions change across organisations. The cost driver
is document review and operational validation, not the interval algorithm.
Possible delivery models are an integration module or advisory assurance service.
Before investment, test whether the workflow finds actionable gaps beyond
existing expiry alerts and whether responsible teams can resolve them. Measure
review effort, additional actionable boundaries and successful exercise coverage.
These are validation criteria, not claimed market results.
